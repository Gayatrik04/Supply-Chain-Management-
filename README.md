# Supply Chain & Inventory Management Dashboard

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive Supply Chain & Inventory Management Dashboard in Microsoft Excel to analyze business performance across sales, inventory, warehouses, suppliers, and delivery operations. The dashboard transforms raw supply chain data into actionable insights through dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking.

---

## Business Objective

The goal of this project is to monitor key supply chain metrics, improve inventory management, evaluate delivery performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Dataset

The dataset includes supply chain transaction data covering orders, products, suppliers, warehouses, inventory levels, delivery performance, and financial metrics such as revenue, cost, and profit.

## Key Insights

- Total revenue reached ₹11.5M, generating ₹3.1M in profit.
- Achieved a healthy gross profit margin of 27.2%.
- 78.4% of orders were delivered on time, while 21.6% experienced delays.
- Average shipping time remained below 6 days (5.98 days).
- The North region contributed the highest share of revenue.
- Warehouse B maintained the largest inventory volume.
- Electronics emerged as the top revenue-generating category.
- Healthy inventory accounted for over 80% of stock levels, indicating effective inventory management.
---

## Business Recommendations

* Maintain optimal inventory levels for high-demand products.
* Improve logistics processes to reduce delivery delays.
* Focus on top-performing regions and product categories.
* Strengthen partnerships with high-profit suppliers.
* Monitor warehouse inventory regularly to prevent stock shortages.
